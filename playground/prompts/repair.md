# Standard repair prompt

This is the single repair opportunity. Read only the attached first-pass check results for your candidate. Repair product or verification failures without changing the product brief or hiding a failing check. Re-run affected tests and build, update current docs and decision records if your condition uses them, and write exact final commands and results to `evidence/final.json`. Do not inspect the sibling candidate, oracle body, condition map, blind scorecards, or prior experiments.
